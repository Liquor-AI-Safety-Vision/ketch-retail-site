## Nguyen nhan goc: CUDA EP tra gia moi khi batch shape doi

Do bang `dev/tools/diag_gpu_batch.py` tren chinh L40S nay:

```
alternating batch shapes (1,2,3,4)  median = 154.4 ms
fixed shape b=1                     median =   4.2 ms
steady b4 = 13.2 ms  -> 3.3 ms/anh
```

CUDA EP lam lai per-shape setup moi lan batch size thay doi, **ke ca voi shape da
warm truoc do**. `cudnn_conv_algo_search=HEURISTIC` khong giup (150 vs 154 ms), nen
day khong phai algo search ma la re-allocation workspace.

Pipeline cu co `pose_batch_size_avg` dao dong 1.06-1.80 -> gan nhu moi lan infer deu
tra gia ~150 ms. Khop voi 105-133 ms do duoc trong pipeline.

Vi vay `SharedPoseService` gio pad moi batch len dung `max_batch` de shape khong doi.
`Embedder` trong `backend/src/perception/stream_track.py` von da lam dieu nay (`REID_BATCH`), va do
la ly do `embed_reid::infer` chi lech 1.6x so voi raw trong khi pose lech 19x.

## Batching that (batch > 1) khong giup: GPU da gan bao hoa, khong co idle de hap thu

Cau hoi dat ra: neu `--pose-max-batch 1` nghia la khong batch gi ca, co dang di
nguoc triet ly gom-nhieu-camera-vao-1-lan-goi cua uae-main khong?

Tra loi: dung la batch=1 tat han co che batch that. Nhung batching kieu uae-main chi
co loi khi **GPU dang ranh, cho viec** - gom nhieu request de lap day thoi gian cho
do. O day GPU da **~79% ban** (do bang `nvidia-smi` lien tuc trong luc chay steady
state, VRAM chi dung 6.2/46 GB) ngay ca khi goi tung anh mot. Khong co idle time
mien phi de hap thu do tre cho gom batch.

Kiem chung bang cach tang `pose_batch_wait_s` (cho lau hon de gom du request that,
khong phai chi don gia):

| batch_wait_s | batch size that | FPS | batch_wait (chi phi cho) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 ms | 2.22 | 43.15 | 5.1 ms |
| 20 ms | 2.63 | 42.20 | 18.8 ms |
| 50 ms | 2.95 | 38.79 | 38.2 ms |
| 100 ms | 3.41 | **26.67** | 100.3 ms |

Batch size that co tang (batching dang thuc su xay ra), nhung don dieu **cang cho
lau cang te**: chi phi cho gom batch tang nhanh hon phan tiet kiem duoc tu infer
theo batch lon. Nguyen ly: batching chi thang khi bottleneck la **so lan goi**
(call overhead); o day bottleneck la **ban than compute**, da duoc chung minh qua
GPU util 79% va qua thu nghiem CUDA MPS ben duoi. Vi vay `max_batch=1` la lua chon
dung cho tinh huong hien tai, du nghe nguoc philosophy ban dau.

Batching **gia** (pad-to-fixed-shape) van co loi vi no khong them do tre cho -
chi la don them anh gia vao cung 1 lan goi da co san, khong lam cham request that.
Do la ly do buoc "fixed-shape pose batch" o tren giup, con "tang batch_wait_s" thi
khong.

Moi camera tu tao mot ONNX session ReID, nam trong `BOOTSTRAP_LOCK` -> 8 camera

giu. Gio session ReID duoc load mot lan moi process, moi camera van giu buffer
(`_pad_batch`) va `last_profile` rieng nen khong co race.

`::zones` (6.05 s/cam) la parse JSON + tinh hinh hoc, chay doc lap moi camera du
cung file - co the cache nhung loi ich toi da chi ~20 s tren tong 135 s.

## Thu nghiem khong hieu qua (da loai bo)

- **`cv2.dnn.blobFromImage`** thay cho cvtColor+transpose+divide: bit-exact nhung
  preprocess van 4.61 ms so voi 4.63 ms, FPS khong doi -> da revert.
- **`cudnn_conv_algo_search=HEURISTIC`**: 150 vs 154 ms, khong dang ke.
- **CUDA MPS** (`nvidia-cuda-mps-control -d`): chenh lech FPS trong khoang nhieu
  (~3%) o ca w2/w4/w8, xac nhan bang log MPS co client that ket noi. GPU da bao
  hoa compute nen MPS (giam overhead context-switch) khong giup duoc nhieu.
- **Doi pose model n/s/m** (`yolo26n/s/m-pose.onnx`): raw speed chi lech 4.93 vs
  5.00 vs 5.71 ms o batch=1 - L40S khong compute-bound o quy mo nay, doi model nho
  hon khong dang cong suc.
- **TensorRT EP**: co san trong `get_available_providers()` nhung thieu
  `libnvinfer.so.10` tren instance, tu dong fallback ve CUDA EP. Chua thu vi can
  cai dat them va rui ro version-mismatch giua TensorRT/CUDA/onnxruntime.
- **Tang `batch_wait_s` de batch that** (xem muc tren): don dieu lam te hon.
